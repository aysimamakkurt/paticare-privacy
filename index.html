PatiCare — Privacy policy

Luna · aysimaakkurt@gmail.com

Last updated: 2026-10-05

PatiCare is a pet care record app provided by Luna. Pet names, species, birth dates, photos, vaccination/medication records, weights, daily dose statuses, vet appointments, clinic/vet details, notes and selected documents are used to track care. This version has no user accounts, advertising or analytics tracking system.

Records are kept in app storage on your device; this version does not automatically upload care records to Luna servers or sync them between devices. On the web, data is stored in your browser. Your operating system’s backup and device security settings also apply. Records and CSV files are not separately encrypted by the app.

Photo and file pickers are used to add only the photos and documents you select. Camera permission is for taking photos; notification permission is for local reminders. Location, contacts and the microphone are not used for care tracking. You can change permissions in device settings. Reminder text may appear on the lock screen.

You initiate PDF, CSV or document sharing and choose the destination app. The recipient app or service’s privacy rules apply. CSV backups contain records and notes, but not profile photos or document files. If you email support, your address and message are processed through the email service to respond to your request; your app records are not attached automatically.

“Delete all app data” in Privacy and data removes records, language/theme preferences and photo/document copies kept in persistent app storage, and cancels PatiCare notifications. Gallery originals, exported backups/PDFs and copies sent to recipients are not deleted; remove these separately. If cleanup fails, a warning is shown and you can retry.

Data stays on your device until you delete it or app storage is cleared. Clearing browser data may remove web records. Restoration from system backups depends on your device settings. You can view, correct, delete and export records as CSV in the app. The app does not provide veterinary advice or official health certificates.

This policy will be updated when new data-processing features are added. For privacy questions or requests, contact the support address above. Avoid emailing sensitive documents that are not needed for support.
